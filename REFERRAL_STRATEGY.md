# Referral hypothesis · defer until retained use

Start with **voluntary introductions, no cash and no incentive for reviews**. Ask a team only after two independently completed engagements: “If another studio asks how you handle this handoff, you're welcome to share our fictional walkthrough. Please don't send us their contact details without permission.” Recipient chooses whether to start a conversation.

No address-book import, invite blasts, public leaderboard or purchased traffic. For a pilot, ask for an optional organization-level “how did you hear about us?”; store consent/source, not a personal social graph. Measure introduced → qualified → activated → retained with small denominators. Stop if self-referrals, spam reports, excessive support or inaccurate promises appear.

A future workspace credit is **not implemented or offered**. Before any incentive: both founders approve budget, fraud checks (one eligible unrelated settled workspace; returns/refunds disqualify), cap/expiration/dispute rules, disclosures, tax/regional laws, provider terms and consent/opt-out. No cash payouts, affiliate contracts, review incentives or retrospective account credits without legal/payment review and explicit authorization.
