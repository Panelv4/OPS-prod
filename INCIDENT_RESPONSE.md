# Incident response · two-founder draft

Name one technical incident lead and one communications/privacy lead, plus an alternate and qualified legal contact, before pilot. Establish a private incident channel outside the affected service. No real incident or notification is alleged here.

| Signal | First containment | Verify before restoring |
|---|---|---|
| Possible cross-workspace exposure or account compromise | Restrict affected routes/workspaces at ingress; preserve minimized evidence; revoke affected sessions, tokens and access with authorized operator | Identify actual rows, recipients and time range; role/tenant regression; inspect logs without copying content into chat |
| Untrusted or mismatched billing event | Keep live off; pause entitlement mutations if necessary, retain verified event IDs; never promote from redirect or screenshot | Reconcile test customer/subscription/invoice/charge and period, net after refund, duplicate/out-of-order events; no manual charging |
| Mail stalled / unexpected repeat | Stop worker or outbound relay, not DB; inspect aggregate oldest due/failed/expired, TLS and sender state | Fresh synthetic code in approved catcher/inbox, no secrets in logs. Old expired codes never re-sent. Confirm post-SMTP/pre-commit duplicate window |
| Workflow noise, wrong task or repeated action | Disable workflow, pause worker on wider issue; inspect status/cursor/step numbers | Check approval intent, source-row scope and idempotency. Don't reset cursor blindly. Test isolated copy before any manual requeue |
| DB unavailable, wrong migration or corrupt data | Put write paths in maintenance; preserve current DB; block worker/egress on restore | Independent restore + journal checks, balances and file samples, role checks and post-backup reconciliation. Controlled DSN switch only |
| Vault compromise/loss | Restrict access, disable provider egress and sending; rotate downstream provider credentials. Preserve old key in controlled custody for re-encryption | Re-encrypt/test on isolated copy, drain or invalidate queued secrets, revoke auth material and verify recovery before destroying an old key |

**Triage clock:** record UTC detection/awareness, reporter, impacted environments/functions, severity and confidence. P0 = credible tenant/financial/credential exposure or severe data loss: page both founders immediately, contain first. P1 = material degradation (worker stalled, recovery unavailable), P2 = isolated noncritical defect. Distinguish observed impact from estimates. Keep only necessary redacted IDs/timestamps and chain of custody; never post bodies, finance values or tokens in a ticket.

**Communications:** determine controller vs processor, affected people and countries, contract/regulator duties, risk, clock and who decides. The EU regulation distinguishes processor notice without undue delay and controller notice where feasible within 72h unless unlikely to pose risk; high-risk individual communications are separate. Seek counsel and document the decision rather than treating that as a universal timer. [1](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng) No automatic public statement, outbound email, compensation, legal commitment or irreversible remediation. Obtain founder approval for external actions without delaying necessary human assessment.

**Closure:** independently verify recovery, list unresolved risks, notify authorized stakeholders by approved channel, preserve evidence under approved retention, hold blameless review with owner/due date, add regression/alert. Tabletop before pilot: rehearse lost SMTP connectivity, expired paid-through while gateway unavailable, tenant complaint, and a restore without sending queued mail. Only the local technical worker failure/retry and restore drills have run, not the organizational tabletop.
