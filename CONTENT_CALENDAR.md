# 30-day zero-budget editorial queue

Draft slots, not instructions to publish automatically. A = technical founder, B = discovery founder; one primary artifact/day, reuse a diagram rather than making three posts. Cap work at ~30 min/day except a single weekly 90-min recording block. Move dates if support or evidence gates slip. Use synthetic labels, captions, transcripts, accessible colors and alt text. No real names/logos, private screenshots or fake comparisons. Founder review before every external post. CTA is a question or opt-in, not an unsolicited DM.

Track channel + artifact + genuine qualified replies, opt-in conversations and return visits voluntarily attributed to it. Don't buy reach, infer identities from visits, equate views with signups or use tracking pixels to reconstruct people.
