USD where identified; per-seat prices and workspace prices are not interchangeable. Recheck locale, term, minimums, onboarding, tax, included apps and renewal at decision time.

| Keep current stack | Spreadsheet + existing task board + accountant's books: marginal license cost may be zero. Cost to change includes data cleanup, training, double entry and trust | No migration risk; familiar finance source of truth | Most important null hypothesis: a weekly spreadsheet update may be good enough. Ask to see a redacted workflow, not private client records |

**Decision:** compete on a short, demonstrable *internal* workflow, predictable workspace price and permission-aware reporting, not depth of statutory accounting, number of apps or speculative AI. If a prospect needs a client portal, two-way books sync or multi-entity finance now, say another tool is a better fit. Record lost reasons instead of promising an integration.
